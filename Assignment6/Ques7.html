Typography and Text Styling Showcase

1. Serif Font Example

Typography plays a crucial role in design and readability. The choice of fonts, sizes, and weights affects how the reader perceives the message. Using a serif font like “Times New Roman” adds a touch of tradition and formality to the text.

2. Sans-serif Font Example

Sans-serif fonts, such as Arial and Helvetica, are modern and clean. They are widely used in web design for clarity and minimalism. Adjusting line-height and letter-spacing improves readability across devices.

3. Monospace Font Example

Monospace fonts like “Courier New” are often used for coding or tabular data because every character takes the same width. This ensures consistent alignment and helps with structured layouts.
